The Red Bicycle

Nadia has a red bicycle named Comet. She rides it to school every Monday, but not on Fridays, because on Fridays her uncle Bram drives her in his blue van.

One Monday the chain of Comet broke near the bakery. Nadia was not sad. She pushed the bicycle to the bakery, and the baker, Mr. Oduya, fixed the chain with a piece of wire.

Nadia gave him a drawing of a horse to say thank you.
