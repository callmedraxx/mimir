The Goat and the Gate

Baba Ike keeps one goat. The goat is called Pepper. In the morning Pepper was in the yard. Then she walked into the garden and ate three cabbages. Baba Ike chased her out of the garden and into the shed. He shut the shed door. At noon his granddaughter Amara opened the shed and led Pepper to the field behind the house. Pepper is in the field now. Baba Ike was angry with the goat in the morning. He is not angry anymore, because Amara gave him a bowl of soup. The garden gate used to be made of wood. Last month Baba Ike replaced it with an iron gate, so now Pepper cannot push it open. Amara was afraid of the goat when she was small. Now she is not afraid of it at all. Baba Ike has kept goats for thirty years.
