The Orchard

Kemi and her brother Tunde look after an apple orchard on a hill near the town of Asaba. Their dog is called Biscuit. Biscuit is no longer young, so he sleeps in the shade most of the day.

Every autumn Tunde climbs the ladder and picks the apples, and Kemi packs them into boxes made of wood and straw. She does not climb the ladder, because she is afraid of falling.

This year a storm broke the old ladder. Tunde was not hurt. The next morning their neighbour came over with a new ladder. He is a carpenter, Mr. Eze. Kemi thanked him with a basket of apples.
