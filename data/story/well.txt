The Well

In the village of Kuru there is one well. It stands in front of the school. Long ago the well had a wooden roof, but the roof burned in a fire. Now the well has a roof made of tin. Two brothers, Sani and Musa, carry water from it every morning. Sani is the older brother. Musa is faster, but he spills a lot of water. Their mother, Hauwa, sells the water to the shops.

One morning the rope broke and the bucket fell into the well. Musa cried. Sani did not cry. He went to the blacksmith, a woman named Ladi, and asked her for a hook. She said the hook would cost ten naira. Sani had only six, so Ladi let him pay the rest later. He tied the hook to a new rope and pulled the bucket out. It was full of mud. Hauwa washed it and gave it back to the boys. Since that day Musa no longer runs with the bucket. He walks.
