The Ferry

A small ferry crosses the river at the village of Ilo. The ferry is called the Duck, and it is made of steel and old planks. Its captain is a woman named Ada. She has worked on the river for thirty years.

Ada no longer takes the ferry out at night, because the river is too dark. One evening a boy named Femi ran to the dock. His mother was ill, and he needed to reach the doctor on the other side. Ada did not say no. She took the Duck across with a lamp on the front.

The doctor came back with them. The next week Femi's mother was well again. She brought Ada a loaf of bread with raisins.
