The Market Stall

Ngozi and her sister Ada sell vegetables at the Saturday market in Okpara. Ngozi grows the vegetables on a farm outside the town. Ada, who is the younger sister, keeps the money and talks to the customers. Their stall is made of bamboo, but the table on it is made of iron. Last year the stall was made of wood, and a goat ate part of it.

For a long time Ada was afraid of the goat, but she is no longer afraid of it. One Saturday a man in a brown hat bought all the tomatoes. He paid Ada with a silver coin instead of money. Ada did not want the coin. The man, who was a teacher from the next village, said the coin was older than the market.

Ngozi took the coin from her sister and gave it to a jeweller named Mr. Obi. He said it was not silver. It was made of tin. Ada laughed, but Ngozi did not laugh.

The next Saturday the teacher came back. He brought three baskets of yams and gave them to Ada to say sorry. The old woman who sells palm oil next to their stall took one basket home. Ada kept the coin anyway. She wears it on a string around her neck now.
