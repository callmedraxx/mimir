The Glass Garden

Mr. Halloran grew tomatoes in a glass house behind the school. The glass house kept the plants warm in spring. His best tomato plant was named Big Ruth.

One morning the children found that Big Ruth had no leaves left. Green caterpillars were eating them. Mr. Halloran did not use poison. Instead he brought in six ladybirds, which eat small insects, and he picked the caterpillars off by hand and carried them to the meadow.

By July Big Ruth was taller than the door. The children sold her tomatoes at the market and bought a new kettle for the teachers.
