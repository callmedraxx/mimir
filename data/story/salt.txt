The Salt Road

Mira Okonde keeps bees on the hill above the village of Tasso. Her oldest hive is called Queen Agnes, and it is painted blue. Mira's brother Lenno does not like bees. He is a potter.

Every autumn Mira trades her honey for salt with the traders who come down the salt road. This year the traders were late. A goat named Pip had eaten the wooden sign at the crossroads, so the traders took the wrong turn.

Lenno made a new sign out of clay and baked it in his kiln. A goat cannot eat a sign made of clay. The traders arrived two days later and paid Mira nine bags of salt.
