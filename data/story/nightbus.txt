The Night Bus

Tomas drives the night bus in the town of Ferno. The bus is old and blue, and the children call it the Whale. Tomas does not like loud music, so the radio on the bus is always off.

One cold night a girl named Suri got on the bus without any money. Tomas did not send her away. He let her ride to the last stop, which is near the hospital. Suri's grandfather, Mr. Bayo, was sick in the hospital.

The next day Mr. Bayo was no longer sick. He came to the bus stop with Suri and gave Tomas a scarf made of wool. Tomas wears it every night now. He never takes it off on the bus.
