The Library Key

The village library has one key. On Monday the key was with Mrs. Okafor, the librarian. She was sick on Tuesday, so she sent the key to the teacher, Mr. Bassey, with her son. Mr. Bassey opened the library on Tuesday and Wednesday. On Thursday he gave the key to a student named Ruth, because he had to travel to Enugu. Ruth lost the key in the grass near the river. On Friday a fisherman named Udo found it and brought it to the chief. The chief gave the key back to Mrs. Okafor, who was well again. So the key is with Mrs. Okafor now. Mr. Bassey is still in Enugu. Ruth was ashamed on Thursday. She is not ashamed now, because Mrs. Okafor thanked her for telling the truth. The library had a thatched roof when it was built. It has a zinc roof now.
