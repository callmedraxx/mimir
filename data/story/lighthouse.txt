The Lighthouse

On a rock near the village of Tarn there is a lighthouse painted white and green. The keeper is an old woman named Marta. She lives in the lighthouse with a grey cat called Pepper. Pepper is not afraid of the sea, but she is afraid of mice.

Marta does not eat fish. She grows beans and potatoes in a small garden behind the lighthouse. Her lamp is made of glass and brass, and it turns all night.

One stormy night the lamp stopped. Marta climbed the stairs without a candle. She found that a moth was stuck in the gears. She took the moth out and let it fly away. The lamp turned again, and a fishing boat called the Swift came home safely.

In the morning the captain of the Swift, Mr. Hale, brought Marta a sack of apples. He wanted to give her money, but she said no.
