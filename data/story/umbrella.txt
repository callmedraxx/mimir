The Blue Umbrella

Nneka owns a blue umbrella. On Monday morning she took it to the market. At the market she gave the umbrella to her friend Dayo, because it started to rain and Dayo had no hat. Dayo carried the umbrella to the bank. He left it in the bank and went home. In the evening a guard named Mr. Felix found the umbrella in the bank. He put it in the lost box. On Tuesday Nneka went to the bank and asked for her umbrella. Mr. Felix gave it back to her. Nneka went home with the umbrella. The umbrella used to be red, but Nneka painted it blue last year. Dayo is now at the market again, buying a hat.
