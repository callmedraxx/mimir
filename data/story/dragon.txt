The Paper Dragon

Ravi and his sister Asha live in the city of Pella. Every year in autumn the city has a kite festival. This year Ravi made a kite shaped like a dragon. It is made of red paper and thin sticks of bamboo. Asha named the kite Ember.

Ravi wanted to fly Ember on the hill, but his mother said the hill was too far. So they went to the park by the river. There was no wind in the morning, so the kite did not fly. Ravi was not angry. He waited.

At noon the wind came, and Ember flew higher than the trees. A judge with a yellow hat gave Ravi a prize: a book about birds. Asha did not win a prize, because her kite broke. Ravi gave her the book.
