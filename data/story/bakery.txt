The Bakery

On the corner of Main Street there is a small bakery. The baker is an old man, Mr. Sola. His wife Bisi sells the bread. She is no longer shy with customers, but he still is.

The bread is made of flour, water and salt. The cakes are made of eggs and sugar. Mr. Sola does not bake on Sundays. On Sundays he goes fishing with his grandson, a boy named Leke.

One Sunday the oven broke. Bisi called her cousin, an electrician. He fixed the oven on Monday morning. Mr. Sola paid him with a cake with cherries on top.
