The Parrot

Mrs. Bello keeps a shop in Jos. In the shop lives a grey parrot called Koko. Koko can say twelve words. Her favourite word is biscuit. Mrs. Bello's son, Tayo, feeds Koko every evening. Tayo does not like the parrot, because it bites his fingers. Last year Koko was kept in a small cage made of wire. Now she lives in a large cage made of wood, which a carpenter named Mr. Audu built. Mr. Audu is Mrs. Bello's neighbour.

One evening Tayo forgot to close the cage. Koko flew out of the shop and sat on the roof of the mosque. Tayo climbed up, but he could not reach her. Mr. Audu brought a ladder. He said the parrot was too high even for the ladder. Then Mrs. Bello held up a biscuit and called her name. Koko came down at once and ate it. After that night Tayo always closes the cage. He still does not like the parrot.
