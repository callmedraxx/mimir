The Bridge

The river Oya runs between the town of Dala and its farms. For many years people crossed the river in a canoe owned by an old man, Baba Tanko. Three years ago the town built a bridge. The bridge is made of concrete, but its rails are made of wood. Baba Tanko no longer carries people across. He fishes under the bridge instead. His granddaughter, Zainab, is an engineer. She checks the bridge every month.

Last month Zainab found a crack in one rail. She told the mayor that the rail was dangerous. The mayor, a short man named Alhaji Dauda, said there was no money. Zainab did not accept this. She bought a plank with her own money and fixed the rail herself. The children who walk to the farm school painted the new rail green. Baba Tanko gave them a fish for it. He did not give one to the mayor.
