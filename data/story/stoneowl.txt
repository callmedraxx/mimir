The Stone Owl

In the square of the town of Belo there is an owl made of stone. People call it Old Watch. It is not a real bird. A sculptor named Ines carved it a hundred years ago.

Every spring, swallows build a nest on its head. This year a boy named Teo wanted to paint the owl gold, but the mayor said no.

Instead Teo painted a sign that says "Please do not climb the owl" and hung it on the fence.
