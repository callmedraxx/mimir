The Paper Lanterns

Every winter the village of Moro holds a lantern night. The lanterns are made of paper and thin wire, and each family makes one.

This year Zara made a lantern shaped like a fish. Her grandfather, who is no longer able to see well, could not help her cut the paper. So he told her stories while she worked, and she listened.

On lantern night it was windy, and many lanterns blew out. Zara's fish did not blow out. The teacher gave the prize to her, a girl of nine. Zara gave her grandfather a cup of hot milk with honey.
