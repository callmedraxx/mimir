The Red Boat

Amara lives in a fishing village called Tobi Bay. Her father, a fisherman named Kunle, owns a small red boat. The boat is made of wood. Every morning Kunle sails out before sunrise and comes back at noon with fish. Amara is nine years old.

She has a grey dog named Pepper. One day a storm broke the mast of the boat. Kunle could not sail, so there were no fish to sell. Amara had an idea. She asked her uncle, a carpenter named Femi, to help.

Femi cut a new mast from a tall bamboo pole. Amara painted the mast yellow. The work took them three days. Kunle thanked Femi with a basket of crabs. On Saturday Kunle sailed again, and Amara and Pepper waved from the beach.
