The Lost Kite

Tomas and his sister Ilse built a kite from red paper and bamboo. They named it Firebird.

On a windy Saturday they flew Firebird in the park, but the string broke and the kite flew away over the river. It landed in the garden of Mrs. Vanek, who grows sunflowers.

Tomas was afraid that she would be angry. She was not angry. She gave the kite back to the children with a jar of plum jam.
