The Night Train

Every night at ten o'clock a train leaves the town of Vell for the sea. The driver is an old woman named Hester Brann, and the train is called the Grey Heron.

One night a boy named Kofi got on the train without a ticket. He wanted to see his father, who works at the lighthouse in Port Ama. Hester did not throw him off the train. Instead she let him sit beside her in the cab and blow the whistle.

At Port Ama, Kofi's father was waiting on the platform. He paid for the ticket with three silver coins.
