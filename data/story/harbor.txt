The Lantern Boat

Sefa lives on the island of Oruna with her grandfather Tavi. Tavi is a fisherman. His boat is called the Moth, and it has a green sail.

Every night Tavi hangs a lantern on the mast, so that other boats can see the Moth in the dark. One evening a storm came from the west. The wind tore the green sail, and the lantern went out.

Sefa ran to the cliff with a jar of fireflies. She held the jar up high. Tavi saw the little lights and steered the Moth safely into the harbor. After that night, the people of Oruna called Sefa the Firefly Girl.
