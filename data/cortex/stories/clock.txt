The Clock of Velloran

Velloran is a small town high in the Ashpine Mountains. In the middle of the town stands a tall stone tower with a big clock. The clock was built by a clockmaker named Imra Batsho. Imra is old now, and her hands shake, so a boy named Dumi helps her. Dumi is twelve years old. He is her apprentice.

Imra keeps a blue owl named Pell. Pell sleeps on the top shelf of the workshop. Pell eats beetles, not mice.

Every morning Dumi climbs the ninety steps of the tower. He oils the gears with goose fat, because Imra says that goose fat does not freeze in winter.

One night the first snow of winter fell on Velloran. In the morning the clock had stopped at seven minutes past three. The baker was late, the school bell did not ring, and the goats were not milked on time. The mayor, a tall man named Orvo Kell, was very angry. He said the clock was too old and must be pulled down.

Dumi climbed the tower with a lamp. Pell flew up beside him. Behind the largest gear Dumi found a walnut. A red squirrel had hidden the walnut in the clock for the winter. The walnut had jammed the gear and stopped the clock.

Dumi took the walnut out of the gear. He did not throw it away. He put the walnut in a small wooden box under the roof, so the squirrel would still have its food. Then he turned the big key forty times, and the clock began to tick again.

The bell rang at noon. The people of Velloran cheered. The mayor said the clock could stay. Imra gave Dumi her own brass screwdriver, which her father had given to her when she was a girl.

From that day, Dumi left one walnut in the wooden box every evening. The squirrel never put anything in the clock again.
