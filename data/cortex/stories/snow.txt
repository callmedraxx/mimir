The Road over Kessel Pass

Anouk drove the mail truck over Kessel Pass every Tuesday. The pass is the highest road in the valley, and in winter it is often closed by snow.

One Tuesday in January the snow was too deep for the truck. Anouk put the letters in a sled and tied the sled to her dog, a big grey dog named Bruno. Bruno pulled the sled for three hours while Anouk walked beside him on skis.

When they reached the village of Lenz, the baker gave Bruno a sausage and gave Anouk hot soup. The letters were dry, because she had wrapped them in her wool coat.
